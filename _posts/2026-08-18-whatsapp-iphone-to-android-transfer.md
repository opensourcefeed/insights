If you're trying to figure out how to transfer WhatsApp from iPhone to Android, you're dealing with one of the trickier device switches out there, mostly because Apple and Google simply don't store WhatsApp backups in the same place. Here's why that happens, what you can expect to keep, and how to move everything — chats, media, and yes, your sticker collection — over to your new Android phone.

## Why iPhone to Android is harder than you think

The core problem comes down to where WhatsApp actually stores your backup. On iPhone, WhatsApp backs up to iCloud. On Android, it backs up to Google Drive. These two systems don't talk to each other, so a backup created on one platform can't simply be restored on the other. There's no official Apple or Google tool that bridges that gap for WhatsApp specifically, unlike the more built-in transfer options available for Android-to-Android or iPhone-to-iPhone moves.

This is exactly why searches for how to transfer WhatsApp data from iPhone to Android turn up so many conflicting answers. Some methods only move contacts. Others only work with text-based chat exports. Very few actually carry over your full conversation history with media and stickers intact.

## What you can (and can't) transfer officially

WhatsApp does now offer an [official way to move your chat history from iPhone to Android](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1295296267926284/?helpref=hc_fnav&cms_id=1295296267926284&dl_redirect=false&draft=true), built right into the app under **Settings > Chats > Transfer Chat History > Transfer to Android**. It's a real improvement over the old export-only option, but it comes with strict requirements that trip a lot of people up:

- Your Android device has to be freshly set up or factory reset — you can't move chats into an Android phone you're already using
- You need Android 12 or newer on the receiving device, and the latest WhatsApp version on both phones
- Both phones have to be connected with a USB-C to Lightning cable and kept unlocked for the entire transfer, and you'll need to scan a QR code partway through
- You're not switching phone numbers, since the same number has to be used on both devices

For anyone with a phone already set up the way they like it — apps installed, settings configured, not interested in a factory reset just to get their chats over — that's a real barrier. There's also still the older "Export Chat" feature, which emails individual conversations to yourself as plain text, but it only moves one chat at a time and stickers generally don't survive the process.

In short, the official route can give you a full, working transfer, but only if you're willing to reset your Android phone and start from a blank slate. If you want your chats to move onto an Android phone you're already using — no reset required — you need a tool built specifically for that.
