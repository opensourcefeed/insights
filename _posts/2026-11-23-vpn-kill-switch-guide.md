A Virtual Private Network (VPN) is designed to keep your online privacy intact. It conceals your authentic IP address and encrypts your online activities by linking your device to a VPN server safely. This requires considerable effort from third parties trying to track your online activities.

Unfortunately, there are times when a VPN stops performing as it should, which can be caused by poor internet connection, server malfunction, and certain settings on your device. When that happens, you may end up using an unencrypted connection.

This is the situation when understanding [how a VPN kill switch works](https://atomicvpn.io/blog/what-is-a-vpn-kill-switch) comes in handy. A kill switch is a safety mechanism that suspends your internet when a VPN fails. It makes sure that no unprotected data is transmitted.

## 1. What Is a VPN Kill Switch?

A kill switch is a VPN feature that secures you from external threats. This function monitors the connection status between the device and the VPN server. If this secure connection fails, the kill switch disconnects your internet access until the VPN connection is back on.

Otherwise, the device can spontaneously connect to the regular internet, thus revealing the real user IP address. Hence, the internet providers and other authorities can monitor the user's activity.

The kill switch solves the problem of revealing the real IP address because it disconnects you from the network in case the VPN goes down. Therefore, this feature is particularly important in situations where the user wants to keep his/her internet usage secret, such as when using public Wi-Fi.

## 2. How Does a VPN Kill Switch Work?

A kill switch keeps checking if your VPN is still working. If the VPN gets shaky or stops, this feature will spot the problem. Then, it blocks certain internet traffic.

The process generally involves three stages:

- **Connection monitoring:** The VPN app looks at the status of its link to the VPN server.
- **Traffic restriction:** If the link stops or drops, the kill switch blocks internet traffic. This helps stop any links from becoming known by mistake.
- **Connection restoration:** When the VPN gets back to working, internet use resumes. It follows what the app's settings say.

The steps you need to follow will change based on your VPN provider and the type of system you use. Some services stop all internet traffic. Others let some apps or local links keep working.

## 3. Different Types of VPN Kill Switches

Not every VPN works the same way when it comes to a kill switch. It is important to know about the different options. This helps you pick a feature that fits your privacy needs well.

**System-level kill switch:** This one stops the whole device for you. [If the VPN stops working](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/vpn-not-connecting/), it will block the internet for all apps. No app can go online until the VPN starts again and is safe to use.

**Application-level kill switch:** This lets you pick the apps you want to stop from using the internet if your VPN stops working. For example, you can block your browser or file-sharing app, but let other programs keep going online.

Some VPNs turn on safety by themselves each time the app links up to a VPN server.

## 4. Why Is a VPN Kill Switch Important?

VPN can stop working for many reasons. This can happen if the Wi-Fi is not stable. It can also stop when you switch to a new network. A busy server can make VPNs drop out. Software problems can also cause this to happen for a short time.

A kill switch helps [stop unexpected data leaks](https://medium.com/@masson.valor/surfshark-vpns-kill-switch-ensuring-no-data-leaks-on-unstable-connections-a7afddeda9f5) when there are breaks in the network. This is good for people who work from home and use their work files online. It is also useful for people who travel a lot and use networks in public places. People who deal with private information will also find it helpful.

It also gives you more safety when you download files, do private research, or open your online accounts. While it can not promise full privacy, it will help you keep your online privacy at a steady level.

## 5. How to Enable a VPN Kill Switch

A lot of VPN applications feature kill switches in safety or connection settings. Specifically, the protocols depend on the provider and the device you are using to access the VPN.

Generally speaking, these are the steps that help how to use it:

- Activate the VPN application and check the settings.
- Identify the kill switch in the security or connection settings.
- Activate or turn on the function.
- Investigate whether it applies globally to the system or only to a few apps.
- Consult the guide issued by the particular provider and learn what happens to your internet connection when you disconnect.

Moreover, it would be good to check if the feature operates properly according to the provider's recommendations.

## Conclusion

A VPN kill switch is a key security tool. It helps stop your internet traffic from being seen if the VPN connection goes down. Knowing **how a VPN kill switch works** can help you pick the right privacy settings. It also helps you keep your browsing safe. A VPN kill switch is not enough alone, but it works best with a strong VPN, an active kill switch, up-to-date software, and smart online habits. Using all of these gives you better privacy every day.
